### 👋 Welcome to SurgeDM

SurgeDM is the home of **Surge**, a next-generation download manager designed for speed, efficiency, and terminal-centric workflows. Our mission is to provide power users with a seamless, high-performance downloading experience that feels right at home in a modern shell.
